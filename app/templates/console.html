How the bouquet is built

            

              
Service reference 4097:0:1:<sid>:0:0:0:0:0:0:<url>:<name> —
                 the leading number picks the player on the box (1 = DVB, 4097 = servicemp3/GStreamer,
                 5001 = ServiceApp gstplayer, 5002 = ServiceApp/exteplayer3).

              
Every colon inside the URL is written %3a — Enigma2 splits the
                 reference on “:” and a raw colon silently truncates the URL. The display name after the last colon
                 may not contain a colon at all.

              
The SID (4th field) is stable — it is derived from the media id, so the box keeps its
                 favourites, picons and EPG mapping when the bouquet is regenerated.

              
bouquets.tv is edited, never overwritten — our line is replaced,
                 every other bouquet on the box is kept.

              
Based on birdy1974/vu-movie (src/enigma2/index.js) and
                 birdy1974/stalker-proxy-manager (app/services/enigma2_bouquets.py).
